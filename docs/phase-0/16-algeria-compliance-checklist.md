# Algeria Compliance Checklist

Scope amendment (2026-10-06): [ADR-011](../decisions/ADR-011-seller-fulfilled-marketplace.md) and [revised scope](22-scope-phasing.md) control this historical baseline. Warehouse, fleet, platform-owned stock, platform goods/COD custody, and unconditional settlement requirements are OUT_OF_SCOPE. Seller stock reservations, seller order processing, delivery visibility, and applicable controls remain required. Authorized safe implementation may proceed; excluded capabilities and older blanket implementation stops do not create release blockers.

**Phase:** 0 — architecture and validation  
**Review date:** 2026-10-02  
**Status:** Draft for qualified Algerian legal, tax, accounting, payments, and privacy review  
**Applies to:** marketplace operator, customer web/mobile, Seller Center, Admin, Customer Service, WMS, courier, operations, finance/settlement, and every provider that processes marketplace data

> **Not legal or accounting advice.** This document is an engineering control checklist derived from the cited primary sources. It is deliberately conservative and is not a substitute for advice from qualified Algerian counsel, the tax/accounting advisers, the Centre National du Registre du Commerce (CNRC), the Autorité Nationale de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (ANPDP), the Banque d’Algérie, an acquiring bank, or another competent authority. No production launch may rely on this draft alone.

## 1. Legal baseline and an important correction

The project brief refers to “Algerian Law 18-05” in both e-commerce and data-protection contexts. These are separate regimes:

- **Law No. 18-05 of 10 May 2018** concerns electronic commerce. Its official text is published in [Official Journal No. 28](https://www.commerce.gov.dz/media/reglementation/source/commerce-electronique/f2018028.pdf).
- **Law No. 18-07 of 10 June 2018** concerns processing personal data. Its official text is published in [Official Journal No. 34](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2018/F2018034.pdf).
- **Law No. 25-11 of 24 July 2025** amended Law 18-07, including duties concerning a data-protection delegate, processing registers, and automated processing-operation logs. The amendment is in [Official Journal No. 48](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025048.pdf).
- **Executive Decree No. 19-89 of 5 March 2019** specifies preservation and transmission of electronic-commerce transaction records. See the [Ministry of Commerce record](https://commerce.gov.dz/fr/reglementation/decret-executif-n-deg-19-89-du-5-mars-2019) and [official text](https://www.commerce.gov.dz/fr/telecharger/reglementation/888/article).
- **Law No. 26-02 of 17 February 2026** now supplies the general framework for electronic trust services and electronic identification and repeals Law 15-04 while preserving its implementing texts until replacements are published. See [Official Journal No. 14](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026014.pdf). Its impact on site authentication, contract evidence, seals, timestamps, and provider selection requires current counsel review.

The checklist uses `BLOCKER`, `REQUIRED`, `VALIDATE`, and `POST-LAUNCH`:

- `BLOCKER`: evidence and accountable approval are required before public production traffic.
- `REQUIRED`: build or operating control required for launch readiness, subject to professional confirmation.
- `VALIDATE`: law, scope, implementation detail, or responsible party must be confirmed; do not infer the answer.
- `POST-LAUNCH`: recurring duty with an owner, cadence, and retained evidence.

## 3. Entity, domain, hosting, and seller eligibility

## 8. Commercial transaction records and CNRC transmission

Executive Decree 19-89 requires an electronic transaction register containing the contract, invoice (or equivalent), and acknowledgements for delivery, return, or recovery. 1. Algerian counsel’s signed compliance memorandum and responsibility matrix.
2. Accountant/tax adviser’s signed invoicing, tax, ledger, settlement, COD, and retention memorandum.
3. Marketplace and first-party merchant corporate/registration evidence.
4. Seller due-diligence policy and sample approved/rejected cases using synthetic data.
5. `.com.dz`, NIC-DZ, CNRC domain-deposit, and e-supplier evidence.
6. Hosting/data-centre contract, location attestation, topology, processor/subprocessor register, and DR/backup location evidence.
7. ANPDP declaration/authorization receipts, DPO notification, processing register, operation-log design, transfer approvals, and privacy notices.
8. Counsel-approved Arabic/French terms, privacy notice, returns/refunds, shipping, seller, prohibited-goods, complaints, and marketing policies.
9. Payment/acquirer authorization and certification evidence, provider security assessment, and reconciliation acceptance test.
10. COD custody/reconciliation procedure with a successfully reconciled end-to-end rehearsal.
11. CNRC transaction-record export acceptance and submission contingency procedure.
12. Category ruleset with cited sources, effective dates, approvers, tests, and re-evaluation evidence.
13. Restore test, disaster-recovery exercise, security assessment, penetration test, privacy exercise, and incident tabletop reports.
14. Traceability report mapping every checklist item to owner, implementation control, test, evidence, and approval.

## 14. Open professional-review questions

- Which party is legally the e-supplier for each marketplace transaction, and which identity/details must appear at product, cart, contract, invoice, and complaint stages?
- Does every third-party seller require its own e-commerce registration/domain/page formalities, or can a seller operate within the marketplace operator’s compliant domain and record framework?
- What exactly constitutes “hosted in Algeria” for origin, API, static assets, CDN/WAF, DNS, logs, monitoring, backups, remote administration, and disaster recovery?
- What authentication mechanism satisfies Law 18-05 art. 8 for this site?
- Which current CNRC technical specifications, credentials, schemas, and submission channel apply under Decree 19-89?
- What retention period applies to each document/data class when commerce, tax, accounting, consumer, privacy, payments, employment, and litigation requirements overlap?
- Which return/refund rules are statutory minimums for each category and scenario, and how are working days and original-packaging conditions interpreted?
- Can the marketplace integrate a 2025-regime PSP directly, or must electronic checkout still be provided through a bank/Algérie Poste/acquirer arrangement under Law 18-05?
- Does marketplace settlement or any wallet/escrow-like function create payment-service, money-transmission, AML/CFT, or other regulated obligations?
- Which ANPDP declarations, authorizations, impact assessments, DPO notifications, and international-transfer approvals are required before pilot and production?
- Which incident-notification rules/timers apply to the private marketplace after Law 25-11, including the relationship between Law 18-07 art. 43 and new Title V bis provisions?
- Are courier photo, signature, GPS, call recording, seller identity documents, and COD/fraud profiles permitted, and with what notices, bases, access, and retention?
- Which Law 26-02 trust service, electronic-identification, website-authentication, qualified seal/signature, or qualified timestamp requirements apply to the public site, customer confirmation, invoices, CNRC records, and regulator submissions? Only a qualified electronic signature is equated to a handwritten signature under art. 7; ordinary checkout must not be described that way without counsel approval.
- How should Finance Law 2025 art. 207 cash restrictions and implementing rules be applied to marketplace categories, seller types, bundles, deposits, delivery collections, refunds, and mixed carts?
